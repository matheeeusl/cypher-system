**[Armor](/en/rules/combat/#armor)** — Subtracted from incoming damage before it reduces a stat Pool. Physical armor, special abilities, cyphers, and artifacts can all contribute to it cumulatively.

**[Artifact](/en/artifacts/)** — A one-of-a-kind device with a depletion roll instead of a fixed number of uses; more durable than a cypher, but not permanent.

**[Asset](/en/rules/core-mechanics/#modifying-the-difficulty)** — Anything that helps with a task—a tool, a cooperating ally, a favorable circumstance—that eases its difficulty, up to two steps.

**[Cypher](/en/cyphers/)** — A single-use, temporary device—subtle, manifest, or fantastic—that a character carries a limited number of at once and expends when used.

**[Damage Track](/en/rules/recovery-and-healing/#the-damage-track)** — The scale from Hale through Impaired and Debilitated to Dead that tracks how badly hurt a character is, independent of which specific stat Pools are empty.

**[Descriptor](/en/descriptors/)** — The adjective in the character sentence ("I am a _clever_ ...")—who a character is, granting skills and other traits regardless of Type or Focus.

**Edge** ([see](/en/rules/core-mechanics/#initial-cost)) — A flat reduction, one per stat, subtracted from the Pool point cost of using Effort or special abilities on that stat.

**[Effort](/en/rules/core-mechanics/#modifying-the-difficulty)** — Spending Pool points to ease a task, up to six steps, at a cost of 3 points for the first level and 2 for each additional level.

**[Flavor](/en/flavors/)** — An optional customization of a Type: at each tier a character can exchange type abilities for ones from the flavor's list (Combat, Stealth…), tailoring the character to the genre or setting.

**[Focus](/en/foci/)** — The verb in the character sentence ("... who _explores dark places_")—what a character does best, granting new special abilities at every tier.

**[GM Intrusion](/en/rules/experience-and-advancement/#gm-intrusion)** — A complication the GM introduces into the story, offered to the player for 1 XP (2 if they initiate it themselves) or imposed for free after a natural 1.

**Pool** ([see](/en/rules/recovery-and-healing/#taking-damage-and-recovery)) — The pool of points behind each of a character's three stats (Might, Speed, Intellect), spent on Effort and special abilities and lost to damage.

**[Recovery Roll](/en/rules/recovery-and-healing/#recovery-rolls)** — A d6 plus tier, rolled to restore lost Pool points; a character can make up to four per day, each requiring more time than the last.

**[Skill](/en/skills/)** — An area a character can be trained or specialized in, easing tasks in that area by one or two steps respectively.

**[Task Difficulty](/en/rules/core-mechanics/#task-difficulty)** — A number from 0 (no roll needed) to 10 (impossible) that the GM assigns to a task, then modifies by easing or hindering before it's converted to a target number.

**[Tier](/en/rules/experience-and-advancement/#reaching-new-tiers)** — A character's overall power level, 1 through 6, raised through character advancement as XP is spent.

**[Type](/en/types/)** — The noun in the character sentence ("I am a ... _Warrior_")—a character's fundamental nature, granting their core capabilities.

**XP (Experience Points)** ([see](/en/rules/experience-and-advancement/#experience-points-xp)) — Points a player earns from GM Intrusions and other sources, spent on immediate, short-to-medium-term, or long-term benefits, including character advancement.
