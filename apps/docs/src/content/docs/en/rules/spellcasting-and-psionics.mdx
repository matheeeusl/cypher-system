Spells and psionic abilities are special abilities that a character learns on top of their type and focus abilities. This chapter covers how to gain them, how to use them, and how to prepare them. It is a summary of the rules for the table: the character app does not track any of this, so keep the XP and the choices on your sheet and talk it over with your GM.

The flavors most tied to this chapter are <EntityLink kind="flavor" id="magic" /> and <EntityLink kind="flavor" id="modern-magic" />. Cantrips, the small spells a Modern Magic character can learn, work on their own rules (see the <EntityLink kind="ability" id="cantrips" /> ability).

## Learning spells and psionic abilities

You can gain a spell or psionic ability by spending **3 XP** and working with the GM on an in-game story that explains how you learned it. You can learn more by spending another 3 XP for each.

To learn mid-tier and high-tier abilities you must reach a minimum tier _and_ already know an ability from the category below:

| Category  | Minimum tier | Prerequisite         |
| --------- | ------------ | -------------------- |
| Low-tier  | 1            | None                 |
| Mid-tier  | 3            | One low-tier ability |
| High-tier | 5            | One mid-tier ability |

The complete list of special abilities by power grade is in Chapter 9: Abilities of the Cypher System Rulebook.

## Activating spells and psionic abilities

When you use a spell or psionic ability you also pay any Pool point cost it has. You can activate it in one of two ways:

- **Recovery roll.** As an action — or as part of another action, if the ability is an Enabler — you expend a one-action, ten-minute or one-hour recovery roll to activate the ability.
- **Time.** You spend time concentrating deeply, or otherwise using all your actions, to activate the ability. A low-tier ability takes ten minutes, a mid-tier ability an hour, and a high-tier ability ten hours.

## Preparing spells and psionic abilities

If you have a magical focus (like Masters Spells), an explicitly psionic one (like Commands Mental Powers), or some other aspect of your character — a magic flavor — that makes you specialized in magic or psionics, you can **prepare** the abilities you have learned. Preparing takes at least one uninterrupted hour of a suitable activity: studying a spellbook, praying to a god, meditating or concentrating. It usually needs a fresh mind, so do it soon after a ten-hour recovery roll.

When you prepare, you can exchange **up to three** type or focus abilities for spells or psionic abilities you have learned. Both abilities must be in the same tier group: low, mid or high.

For example, you can exchange Far Step (one of your tier 1 Adept abilities) to prepare Ribbons of Dark Matter (a low-tier spell you know). Once you do, you can use Ribbons of Dark Matter normally, but you can use Far Step only by expending a recovery roll or spending time to activate it.
