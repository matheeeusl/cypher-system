# Foci population status

Chapter 5 of `rulebook/cypherrules.pdf` (Old Gus' Unofficial Cypher System
Player's Guide) lists 141 Foci, pages 168–394, one continuous alphabetical
run — there is no genre-agnostic/genre-specific split in this document (see
project chat history; every Focus mixes freely with genre-flavored ones).

This file tracks, per Focus, whether `content/foci/<id>.yaml` and its
referenced `content/abilities/*.yaml` files have been populated with real
rulebook text. Update the relevant row(s) whenever a population pass adds,
finishes, or revises a Focus — this list is the source of truth for what's
left, not tribal knowledge.

Status values:

- `done` — Focus YAML complete, every referenced Ability populated and
  cross-checked against the source PDF.
- `partial` — Focus YAML exists but is incomplete (e.g. only some tiers
  populated).
- `blocked` — a population attempt was made and the tool output was
  blocked before content could be written; needs a retry.
- `pending` — not started.

## Schema gaps found while populating (for whoever picks this up next)

These were flagged rather than silently forced into shape.

1. ~~**Non-binary ability choices.**~~ **Resolved 2026-08-07.**
   `FocusSchema`'s `choice` variant now takes `options: array(...).min(2)`
   plus a `count` field instead of a fixed 2-of-2. Channels Divine
   Blessings' Tier 1 is now populated with it — turns out to be a real
   choose-2-of-**13** (not "eleven" as originally miscounted here; a
   fresh, independent re-extraction of pages 186–188 found 13 named
   sub-abilities under "Blessing of the Gods": Authority/Law/Peace,
   Benevolence/Righteousness/Spirit, Death/Darkness, Desire/Love/Health,
   Earth/Stone, Knowledge/Wisdom, Nature/Animals/Plants,
   Protection/Silence, Sky/Air, Sun/Light/Fire, Trickery/Greed/Commerce,
   War, Water/Sea — the miscount came from undercounting bullet points
   in the source dump).
2. ~~**Per-source tier disagreement.**~~ **Resolved 2026-08-07.** `tier`
   moved from a top-level `Ability` field into each `sources[]` entry, so
   the same ability can correctly report a different tier per Focus/Type
   that grants it. All 212 existing Ability files were migrated; the
   abilities that actually disagreed by tier now carry the real
   per-source values: `surprise-attack` (tier 3 under Collects Bounties
   and Fights Dirty, tier 4 under Hunts), `able-assistance` (tier 6 under
   Descends From Nobility, tier 5 under Entertains), `skill-with-attacks`
   (tier 3 under Helps Their Friends, tier 6 under Pilots Starcraft),
   `deep-reserves` (tier 4 under Abides in Stone, tier 5 under Is a
   Walking Tank), `enhanced-physique` (tier 4 under Helps Their Friends,
   tier 2 under Loves the Void). Everything else shares a matching tier
   across sources.
3. **`action_type` enum doesn't cover every printed tag — open, not yet
   decided.** The schema's 4 values (`action`, `enabler`,
   `action_to_initiate`, `action_to_initiate_or_reinitiate`) were
   validated against Type abilities; Focus abilities print substantially
   more variety. A systematic audit of all 208 abilities populated this
   session (excluding the 3 standard tags `Enabler.`/`Action.`/`Action to
initiate.`) found **15 distinct non-standard tags across 21
   abilities**:

   | Source tag (exact)                                                   | Count | Abilities                                                                                                              |
   | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | `Action to create.`                                                  | 4     | `illusory-disguise`, `major-illusion`, `illusory-selves` (Crafts Illusions), `wall-of-lightning` (Rides the Lightning) |
   | `Action to initiate; action each round to concentrate.`              | 3     | `find-the-hidden`, `remote-viewing`, `see-through-time` (Sees Beyond)                                                  |
   | `Action to create; action to direct.`                                | 2     | `fiery-hand-of-doom`, `fire-servant` (Bears a Halo of Fire)                                                            |
   | `Action to create; action to modify.`                                | 1     | `minor-illusion` (Crafts Illusions)                                                                                    |
   | `Action to create; action to check.`                                 | 1     | `sensor` (Sees Beyond)                                                                                                 |
   | `Action to establish.`                                               | 1     | `use-senses-of-others` (Commands Mental Powers)                                                                        |
   | `Action to establish contact.`                                       | 1     | `telepathic` (Commands Mental Powers)                                                                                  |
   | `Action to initiate; action each round to drain.`                    | 1     | `drain-charge` (Rides the Lightning)                                                                                   |
   | `Action to initiate, one hour to complete.`                          | 1     | `salvage-and-comfort` (Pilots Starcraft)                                                                               |
   | `A few rounds to create the enhanced blaze symbol; action to check.` | 1     | `trail-sensor` (Blazes Paths)                                                                                          |
   | `Action to change.`                                                  | 1     | `controlled-change` (Howls at the Moon)                                                                                |
   | `Action to change back.`                                             | 1     | `beast-form` (Howls at the Moon)                                                                                       |
   | `Action to activate.`                                                | 1     | `electrical-flight` (Rides the Lightning)                                                                              |
   | `Action for touch; enabler for weapon.`                              | 1     | `shock` (Rides the Lightning)                                                                                          |
   | `Enabler for enhancement; action for long-range attack.`             | 1     | `arcane-flare` (Masters Spells)                                                                                        |

   Every current file stores the closest single enum value it could
   (usually `action_to_initiate` for anything starting "Action to..."),
   correct or not. Only 7 of the 21 abilities carry an inline `#` comment
   flagging the approximation (`fiery-hand-of-doom`, `fire-servant`,
   `sensor`, `telepathic`, `trail-sensor`, `use-senses-of-others`,
   `arcane-flare` — grep for `"Note: source tag is"`); the other 14
   (`illusory-disguise`, `major-illusion`, `illusory-selves`,
   `wall-of-lightning`, `find-the-hidden`, `remote-viewing`,
   `see-through-time`, `minor-illusion`, `drain-charge`,
   `salvage-and-comfort`, `controlled-change`, `beast-form`,
   `electrical-flight`, `shock`) were missed during the initial pass and
   do **not** carry a comment yet — this table is the authoritative list
   until that's fixed. No enum/schema change has been made for this yet;
   decide the right shape (more enum values? split into two fields for
   the two genuinely dual-action cases? a raw-tag passthrough field?)
   before touching these 21 files again.

4. **Reference-table content embedded in Focus prose.** Takes Animal
   Shape's Tier 1 ability points at an "Animal Form Minor Abilities
   Table" (a real table of ~20 animals × trained skill × special
   ability) that isn't a Focus, Ability, or any existing reference-table
   category. Same shape as the existing Focus Connections table
   (`content/reference-tables/`) but per-animal-shape rather than
   per-focus. Not modeled; worth a `reference-tables/` entry if/when
   Takes Animal Shape gets (re)populated.
5. ~~**Per-Focus flavor fields with no schema field.**~~ **Resolved
   2026-08-07.** The original vague claim here didn't hold up under a
   direct check (Controls Gravity has neither pattern — its only prose
   is a one-sentence intro; that Focus was never actually claimed to
   have it, but it's confirmed clean) and was corrected below with
   precise citations, then independently re-verified against a
   completely fresh re-extraction (separate `pdfjs-dist` install, no
   reuse of cached data) after a second round of scrutiny — same result
   both times, character-for-character. `FocusSchema` gained
   `additional_equipment` (same shape as the existing `Descriptor`
   field), `connection_options` (same shape as `Descriptor`'s, but
   nullable — most Foci rely on the shared d100 table instead),
   `minor_effect_suggestion`, and `major_effect_suggestion`, all
   nullable. All 25 populated Foci were migrated (null by default); the
   4 that actually have this content — Blazes Paths, Codes Magic Apps,
   Collects Bounties, Is a Walking Tank — now carry the real values.
   The pattern is real, but only in **4 of the 25** populated Foci, and
   it's actually two separate patterns that had gotten merged into one
   bullet in the original report:

   - **A bespoke, Focus-specific 4-option `Connection:` list**, distinct
     from the shared d100 Focus Connections table (`content/reference-tables/`)
     described on page 165 and in `packages/content/CLAUDE.md`'s
     "every Focus shares the same d100 table" line. Present, verbatim,
     in:
     - **Blazes Paths** (p. 178): `Connection:` followed by 4 numbered
       options, e.g. "1. Pick one other PC. For some reason, whenever
       that character stands right next to you, your navigation tasks
       are hindered." ... plus a `Minor Effect Suggestion:` ("You
       restore 2 points to your Speed Pool.") and `Major Effect
Suggestion:` ("An ally or indicated target can immediately move
       up to a short distance.").
     - **Codes Magic Apps** (p. 189): same 3-part shape — `Connection:`
       (4 options, e.g. "1. Pick one other PC. You once created a
       magical app to help get them out of a sticky situation..."),
       `Minor Effect Suggestion:` ("Your next use of a magical app
       cypher within the next hour is eased."), `Major Effect
Suggestion:` ("Your next use of a magical app cypher within the
       next day is eased.").
     - **Collects Bounties** (p. 191): same shape — `Connection:` (4
       options, e.g. "1. Pick one other PC. They accidentally provided
       information to a competing bounty hunter that lost you a quarry
       with a particularly lucrative reward."), `Minor Effect
Suggestion:` ("Your attack against a foe is eased."), `Major
Effect Suggestion:` ("Make an immediate extra attack on your
       target.").
     - **Is a Walking Tank** (p. 270): same `Connection:` (4 options,
       e.g. "1. Pick one other PC. This character protected you from
       harm recently, and you feel indebted to them for saving your
       life.") + `Minor Effect Suggestion:` ("You gain +1 to Armor for
       one round.") + `Major Effect Suggestion:` ("You regain 2 points
       to your Might Pool.") block — but see below, this one has no
       Additional Equipment line.
   - **A distinct, separate `Additional Equipment:` line** — present in
     only 3 of those 4: Blazes Paths ("You have a hatchet, blue dye, and
     a brush—all equipment you use to blaze your trails in the
     wilderness."), Codes Magic Apps ("Computer (laptop or desktop) and
     a smartphone or tablet."), and Collects Bounties ("Heavy leather
     duster (light armor)."). **Is a Walking Tank does not have this
     line** — flag it separately as requested; my earlier report
     conflated it with the other three.

   `FocusSchema` has no field for either pattern — both were dropped,
   not modeled, in all 4 Foci. Worth flagging to whoever owns the
   CLAUDE.md convention note, since it currently states the d100 table
   is shared by _every_ Focus without exception.
