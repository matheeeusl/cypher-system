# Foci population status

Chapter 5 of `rulebook/cypherrules.pdf` (Old Gus' Unofficial Cypher System
Player's Guide) lists 141 Foci, pages 168–394, one continuous alphabetical
run — there is no genre-agnostic/genre-specific split in this document (see
project chat history; every Focus mixes freely with genre-flavored ones).

This file tracks, per Focus, whether `content/foci/<id>.yaml` and its
referenced `content/abilities/*.yaml` files have been populated with real
rulebook text. Update the relevant row(s) whenever a population pass adds,
finishes, or revises a Focus — this list is the source of truth for what's
left, not tribal knowledge.

Status values:

- `done` — Focus YAML complete, every referenced Ability populated and
  cross-checked against the source PDF.
- `partial` — Focus YAML exists but is incomplete (e.g. only some tiers
  populated).
- `blocked` — a population attempt was made and the tool output was
  blocked before content could be written; needs a retry.
- `pending` — not started.

## Schema gaps found while populating (for whoever picks this up next)

These were flagged rather than silently forced into shape.

1. ~~**Non-binary ability choices.**~~ **Resolved 2026-08-07.**
   `FocusSchema`'s `choice` variant now takes `options: array(...).min(2)`
   plus a `count` field instead of a fixed 2-of-2. Channels Divine
   Blessings' Tier 1 is now populated with it — turns out to be a real
   choose-2-of-**13** (not "eleven" as originally miscounted here; a
   fresh, independent re-extraction of pages 186–188 found 13 named
   sub-abilities under "Blessing of the Gods": Authority/Law/Peace,
   Benevolence/Righteousness/Spirit, Death/Darkness, Desire/Love/Health,
   Earth/Stone, Knowledge/Wisdom, Nature/Animals/Plants,
   Protection/Silence, Sky/Air, Sun/Light/Fire, Trickery/Greed/Commerce,
   War, Water/Sea — the miscount came from undercounting bullet points
   in the source dump).
2. ~~**Per-source tier disagreement.**~~ **Resolved 2026-08-07.** `tier`
   moved from a top-level `Ability` field into each `sources[]` entry, so
   the same ability can correctly report a different tier per Focus/Type
   that grants it. All 212 existing Ability files were migrated; the
   abilities that actually disagreed by tier now carry the real
   per-source values: `surprise-attack` (tier 3 under Collects Bounties
   and Fights Dirty, tier 4 under Hunts), `able-assistance` (tier 6 under
   Descends From Nobility, tier 5 under Entertains), `skill-with-attacks`
   (tier 3 under Helps Their Friends, tier 6 under Pilots Starcraft),
   `deep-reserves` (tier 4 under Abides in Stone, tier 5 under Is a
   Walking Tank), `enhanced-physique` (tier 4 under Helps Their Friends,
   tier 2 under Loves the Void). Everything else shared a matching tier
   across sources as of that date; a later session's Focus batch (Absorbs
   Energy through Conjures Bullets) added more real disagreements:
   `skill-with-defense` (tier 6 under Helps Their Friends, tier 3 under
   both Blazes With Radiance and Builds Robots), `enhanced-intellect`
